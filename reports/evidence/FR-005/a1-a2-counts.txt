--- The reconciliation, done properly rather than by eye ---

A-2's expected result is that the per-module counts sum to A-1's N. Summing thirteen numbers
that happen to add up is weak evidence -- it would not detect a test collected under two
packages, or one collected but never run. So the Master collected test *ids* with
unittest's loader, both ways, and compared the sets:

  top-level discover:      655 collected, 655 unique
  sum of per-module:       655 collected, 655 unique
  ids collected twice across per-module runs:   0
  in per-module but not top-level:              0
  in top-level but not per-module:              0

Exact set equality, no duplicates, no asymmetry.

--- A correction to an earlier Master figure ---

An earlier run in this brief was recorded as "Ran 648". That was a misread: the number was
captured from a truncated tail while the loader was reporting 655, and the discrepancy is
what prompted the id-level reconciliation above. There is no missing set of seven tests. The
figure is 655, confirmed by two independent methods. Implementers were told the corrected
baseline so their own deltas are measured against the right number.
