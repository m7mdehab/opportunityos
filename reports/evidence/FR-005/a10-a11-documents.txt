So the tripwire is load-bearing: a validator that stopped rejecting unsupported founder

--- A finding the Master did not expect, recorded because it matters ---

Under the SAME neutralisation, matching/test_artifacts_e2e.py stayed green:

  guard 9 neutralised  -> matching.test_artifacts_e2e : Ran 16 tests, OK

The sixteen end-to-end tests assert that documents ARE produced and that their sentences map
to claims. They do not, and cannot, detect a validator that has stopped refusing -- because a
neutralised validator refuses nothing, so every claim maps and every document renders. The
safety property rests entirely on the tripwire in api/test_api.py, not on the e2e suite.

This is why A-11 was written as a separate claim from A-10 before any code was delegated: a
document that generates because the guard stopped guarding is not a delivered document. If
the two had been one row, this brief could have reported A-10 green with the guard removed
and nothing would have caught it.
