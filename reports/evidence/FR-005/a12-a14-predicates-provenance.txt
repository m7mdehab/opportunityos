--- A-12: the dimension that could not see the founder ---

Scoring the corrected fixture graph against the employment opportunity fixture:

  overall_fit_score = 84.50 (0-100)   decision = QUALIFIED

  dimension                  raw     weight   evidence_refs
  core_skills                1.000   0.35     2
  seniority_and_experience   1.000   0.20     1
  responsibility_scope       0.800   0.15     2      <-- was a flat 0.500 with 0 refs
  domain_fit                 0.550   0.10     1
  geography_and_remote       1.000   0.10     1
  compensation_fit           0.500   0.05     0
  career_trajectory          0.500   0.05     0

Before D2, `responsibility_scope` read four predicate names the graph never projects
(`responsibility.item`, `employment.role_description`, `experience.summary`,
`achievement.description`) against a graph that emits `employment.responsibility` and
`achievement.statement`. For an employment-only founder the only non-orphan in that list was
`service.name`, which belongs to the independent track -- so the dimension returned the
neutral 0.500 with zero evidence refs for every founder, and had done since BRIEF-004.

It now reads what the graph emits and returns 0.800 with two evidence references. At weight
0.15 that is roughly 4.5 points of overall fit score that were previously unreachable no
matter what the founder's history contained.

`compensation_fit` still shows 0.500 with no refs on this fixture: the fixture opportunity
states no compensation, and D2's premium rule is specified to treat unknown compensation as
UNKNOWN rather than as a penalty. That 0.500 is correct behaviour, not a second instance of
the defect.

--- An environment hazard found while doing this, recorded because it wasted an hour ---

A scratch database was left with `alembic_version` reading `0002_match_evaluations` while the
tables themselves no longer existed, so `alembic upgrade head` failed inside 0003's
de-duplication DELETE with `relation "field_provenances" does not exist`, and the suite then
reported 13 errors and a reduced collection of 550 tests. Nothing in this brief caused it:
some test in the suite drops the schema without resetting the alembic version table. Dropping
and recreating the database made the suite green at 599. This is pre-existing, is not fixed
here, and is the most likely explanation for the single unreproducible
"FAILED (failures=1)" recorded in a0-a1-baseline.txt.


--- CORRECTION after independent verification ---

The figures above (responsibility_scope 0.800 with two evidence refs) were measured before
the D1 and D3 council repairs landed. Re-measured by the verifier at the merge head, the same
named fixture yields **0.95 with three evidence refs** (a-resp1, a-resp2, a-ach). The fixture
gained an achievement assertion in a later commit.

The claim's binding -- raw_score > 0.50 with non-empty evidence_refs -- holds comfortably
either way, and the before figure (a flat 0.500 with zero refs) is unchanged. But 0.800 was
stale relative to the head being merged, and is corrected here rather than left to be
discovered. The report quotes the corrected figure.
