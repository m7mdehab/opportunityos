## Objective

Make the opportunity detail drawer explain the existing match decision in clear, separate terms: eligibility, Capability Fit, Preference, and Confidence. Show the evidence already returned by the detail API so the Founder can understand score components, strengths, gaps, and unknowns without treating the Recommended composite as the explanation.

## Contract

- Keep the four concepts separate in the UI: qualification/eligibility is a decision; Capability Fit measures skills and experience against the work; Preference reflects stated preferences; Confidence describes evidence completeness/reliability.
- Render available 0–100 preference and confidence scores, and their supplied rationale factors. Keep unavailable/legacy values visibly unavailable rather than defaulting them to zero or deriving them from another score.
- Retain per-dimension fit scores and rationales, and clearly group strengths, verified gaps, and unknowns. Do not describe unknown evidence as a negative finding.
- Keep qualification constraint outcomes and their explanations distinct from scoring. Preserve `review_required` as uncertainty, not rejection.
- Use readable labels and short definitions. Do not invent calibration, score bands, weights, confidence factors, or explanations that are absent from the API payload. Identify existing bands/weights as provisional where they are shown.
- Extend the TypeScript detail contract and synthetic mock only to represent existing API fields. Add/adjust synthetic browser coverage for populated, unknown/null, and legacy detail payloads.
- Do not read or use private Founder profile/CV/truth-pack files or data. Use synthetic fixtures only.
