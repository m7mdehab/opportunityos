# W7.2 readiness — One-click triage and Undo

**Decision:** `READY / ORDER-LOCKED`

W7.1 is integrated and pushed at `044deb2`. Its report names W7.2 as the next brief slice. The database already records state transitions with `from_state`, `to_state`, timestamp, and an internal event ID; however, there is no safe restore operation or reversal event yet. The default feed excludes Founder-attested `submitted` rows, so Undo of Mark Applied must also withdraw that status from the active-feed predicate without deleting the historical attestation row.

Current mock actions persist tracker state and timeline events separately. W7.2 will make Undo and direct card actions follow the same state-write success boundary, test stale Undo against intervening activity, and keep private/event identifiers out of visible copy.

The scope is limited to the immediately preceding Save, Mark Applied, or Reject action. Bulk triage and arbitrary restore of older closed jobs remain separate work. No schema migration or external environment is required.
