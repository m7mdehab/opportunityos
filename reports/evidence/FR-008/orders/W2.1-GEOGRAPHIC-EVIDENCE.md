# W2.1 — Geography decisions require structured evidence

## Deliverable

Align employment geography qualification with FR-008's evidence rule. A legacy `GeographicEligibility.status` or its prose `reason` is a derived label, not evidence of a mandatory applicant restriction and not proof that the Founder can satisfy all location conditions.

Resolve from the existing structured `remote_scope`, `remote_scope_regions`, `location_country`, work mode, and verified Founder residence/work-authorization assertions first. When those fields do not resolve the restriction, return review-required/uncertain for every legacy geography status, including `eligible`, `excluded`, and `ineligible`; do not parse `GeographicEligibility.reason` or let a bare status create a hard failure.

Keep these rules:

- Worldwide remote resolves without treating the employer's country as the applicant's required location.
- A remote region that excludes Egypt remains review-required; it is not a hard failure.
- A named location with no verified Founder compatibility or incompatibility remains review-required.
- A verified negative authorization may produce a hard failure only when structured job evidence establishes mandatory physical presence in that same jurisdiction.
- A verified Founder residence that differs from a mandatory onsite location is not itself proof of inability or unwillingness to relocate. Keep that decision review-required unless an explicit verified Founder-side restriction exists; do not invent a relocation preference or predicate in this slice.
- Preserve explicit, evidenced mandatory work-authorization handling and unrelated qualification behavior.

Add synthetic regression coverage for status-only legacy exclusion, structured worldwide scope overriding a conflicting legacy exclusion, status-only legacy inclusion with unknown scope, remote foreign-employer country with no applicant restriction, explicit onsite location differing from verified residence, same-location onsite, and the existing explicit negative-authorization cases. Never print canonical Founder values.
