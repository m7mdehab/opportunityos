# W0.2-GOLD-REVIEW-HARNESS — Privacy-safe review contract and sampler

## Deliverable

Add a deterministic, privacy-safe data contract and stratified selection utility for the FR-008 real-job gold set. The utility accepts opaque opportunity IDs and categorical strata supplied by a later read-only corpus selection step; it must not load job descriptions, Founder data, database credentials, or hosted services. It must select unique IDs deterministically from a supplied seed, guarantee the requested per-stratum minimum or fail explicitly, and avoid committing any real opportunity IDs or Founder labels.

Add validated review labels for the brief's five actionability choices, capability-fit bands, geography/seniority/required-skill correctness, expected target-family tier, and a short reviewer rationale. Serialization must be versioned and strict; it must not accept or emit job text, Founder profile content, or unlisted fields. Do not fabricate a Founder label or implement ranking calibration in this slice.

The later real-corpus sample capture is intentionally a separate read-only order after the family/geography classification inputs are stable. This harness must support it without changing the current database or adding jobs to Git.
