## Objective

Implement the brief's separate `confidence_score` (0–100): how complete and reliable the evidence is behind a match decision. Confidence must decrease when posting evidence or relevant Founder evidence is incomplete, ambiguous, stale, or weak. Missing information must remain unknown and must not be turned into a negative match decision.

Expose the score and its named factors in persisted evaluation detail and the opportunity detail API. Legacy evaluations without detail must remain readable and expose null confidence. Keep `overall_fit_score` limited to capability fit and qualification decisions; missing-evidence uncertainty is reported through confidence and diagnostics, not subtracted from capability fit. Do not change qualification outcomes, preference scoring, schema, calibration, or gold-set labels in this slice.

The confidence factors must cover the seven brief signals: description completeness; location/remote-scope clarity; experience-requirement clarity; reliability of required-skill extraction; compensation completeness; source freshness/strength; and completeness of Founder evidence for relevant dimensions. Any heuristic thresholds or factor aggregation introduced here must be named, deterministic, inspectable, and explicitly uncalibrated. No real Founder or private profile data may be read or used.
