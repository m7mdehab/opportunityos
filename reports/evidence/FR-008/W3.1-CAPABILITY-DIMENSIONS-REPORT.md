## Implemented dimensions

Employment evaluations now expose separate signals for verified employment role-family history, skill evidence, verified professional experience months, seniority level, responsibility alignment, domain relevance, and education/certification alignment. Role-family history is reported separately from total tenure so the two do not imply the same evidence. The existing combined seniority-and-experience key remains as a zero-weight compatibility projection that points consumers to `experience_fit` and `seniority_fit`.

Title-family capability uses verified employment-title assertions. Verified target-role assertions produce the distinct `target_role_family_preference` dimension, so a preference is not presented as employment history. The education/certification dimension reads only verified positive education qualifications and verified completed certifications; a missing or ambiguous Founder record stays unknown, and planned/in-progress/expired certifications do not count as held.

## Weight and score boundary

The suggested FR-008 weights were not applied. New role-history, seniority, and education/certification dimensions carry zero placeholder weight pending real-label calibration; those zeroes mean uncalibrated, not irrelevant. The existing capability and preference weights remain finite and sum to 1.0.

`overall_fit_score` still includes the legacy geography/work-mode, compensation, career-trajectory, and target-role-family preference contributions. W3.2 owns an explicit preference-score output and the removal of those preference signals from capability fit. This W3.1 slice does not claim that the final FR-008 score separation is complete.
