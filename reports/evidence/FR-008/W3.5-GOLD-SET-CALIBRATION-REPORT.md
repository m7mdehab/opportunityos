# W3.5 — Gold-set calibration evaluator

**Status:** `PARTIAL / REVIEWABLE / CALIBRATION PENDING`

## Implemented

Added `matching/ranking_calibration.py`, a deterministic offline evaluator for typed W0.2 gold-review labels and caller-supplied predictions. It computes hard-ineligible precision and false-positive/false-negative counts, reviewer-marked geography/seniority/skill correctness, target-tier accuracy, Recommended apply/likely-versus-probably-skip pairwise concordance, capability-fit Spearman rank correlation, and mean fit scores by Founder fit band. It reports the brief's 100%, 95%, and 90% targets and keeps unavailable measures null with a limitation.

Reports contain aggregate counts and stable one-way hashed review tokens for eligibility false positives/negatives. They omit raw job IDs, reviewer rationales, job text, and Founder/profile content. The 150-item count alone does not attest a real Founder-reviewed dataset; the caller must explicitly attest that provenance before `minimum_sample_met` can become true.

No production scoring or ranking parameter was tuned. The evaluator does not load jobs, profile material, a database, or the network.
