--- A finding from the real-stack phase: the shipped template cannot produce a CV ---

The template loads cleanly and truth_check reports it valid, but compiling a
tailored document from it is refused by the claim validator. Two guards stack:

  truth/validator.py:368  refuses a composite claim whose cited evidence records
                          are not relationally linked
  truth/validator.py:378  refuses any claim whose material terms are absent from
                          that evidence

Measured against the shipped template:
  cv            claims=3 rejected=1
     e.g. Professional background as Senior Widget Engineer with verified competen
       -> composite claim combines independent evidence records without an establishing graph relation
  cover_letter  claims=2 rejected=2
     e.g. I am writing to express my interest in the Principal Backend Architect p
       -> claim contains material terms absent from evidence: aligns, am, architect, backend, background, clou

This is the guard working, not failing: the API returns 409 with the findings and
NEVER produces document bytes for a rejected claim. The system refuses to put an
unsupported claim in a document carrying the founder's name.

It was deliberately NOT forced closed. The only way to make the template pass is
to write the compiler's vocabulary into the founder's evidence text -- phrasing
evidence to match generated prose -- which in a project whose first hard rule is
'never fabricate a claim about the founder' is exactly backwards. An agent was
mid-task on that approach and was stopped. Reconciling the compiler with the
validator is the first item in the report's section 10.
