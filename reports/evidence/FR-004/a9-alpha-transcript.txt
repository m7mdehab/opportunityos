=== CORRECTION appended 2026-09-02 after Overseer review — see REPORT-FR-004.md Erratum 1.1 ===

The heading "--- the logged-in feed, over really-polled data ---" above is WRONG. The raw
output under it is unaltered and accurate; the Master's description of it was not.

Those 138 rows are test-database residue, not polled data. Every one carries a synthetic id
(opp-uq-*), source_id "src-1", a https://example.com/ source_url and "Acme Corp" as the
organization. alpha.py takes its database from OPPORTUNITYOS_DB_URL in the env file, and the
temp env file used for this run pointed at opportunityos_test, so `up` attached to the
already-populated test database instead of a database of its own. The "--- the measured
number ---" line below it (139 fetched, 1 qualified) is therefore the synthetic pack scored
against that residue, not a measurement over anything real.

No live poll through the poll_source -> persist_batch -> evaluate_new seam is evidenced
anywhere in FR-004. FR-005 D2 carries that proof, and gives alpha.py its own database.
