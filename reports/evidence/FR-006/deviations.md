# BRIEF-FR-006 — Deviations

Maintained as they happen, not reconstructed at the end. One row each in the report per
`docs/AGENT_EXECUTION_PROTOCOL.md` §8.

| # | What | Why | Authorised by | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Track 0's `F2` was dispatched to an implementer rather than executed by the Master. | The brief calls Track 0 "sequential, Master"; the protocol's §0 says the Master never implements. The protocol is the later authority and the brief itself says it executes under the protocol. | Master, reading protocol §0 over brief §2 Track 0. | `dev_env.py` is subject to the same tier-1/2/3 verification as every other deliverable instead of being self-certified by its author. |
| 2 | Brief nodes **E2** and **E3** were merged into a single work order `E23`. | Both write `docs/SOURCE_REGISTRY.yaml` and `docs/SOURCE_EVIDENCE.md`, and `E1` appends to the same two files concurrently. Both nodes' acceptance rows survive unchanged. |
| 3 | Migration `0004` **retains** `founder_filter_settings` instead of replacing it with `founder_facets`. | The brief says `0004` "replaces `founder_filter_settings` with `founder_facets`". The ten policy filters carry a `hide` / `rank_only` / `label_only` mode vocabulary; facets carry `include` / `exclude` / `off`. These are different semantics, and claim A-18 (carried from FR-005 A-13) requires the filter vocabulary to keep working — including the Overseer's own Appendix-5 decision that `target_roles` defaults to `rank_only`, a mode facets cannot express. Collapsing the two tables would have deleted the vocabulary that the Overseer's decision is written in. | Master. | Two tables instead of one. `/api/filters` survives; `/api/facets` is new. The brief's stated intent — every extracted attribute is a facet with include/exclude/off and nothing hides by default — is delivered in full. |
| 4 | The whole of migration `0004` — including columns and tables that work orders A2, C1, C2, D2 and E4 will populate — is specified by the Master and written by work order **A1** alone. | The brief has five deliverables sharing one revision. Five implementers editing one migration file in five worktrees produces either a merge conflict or five revisions. | Master, per protocol §4 (the Master resolves integration). | A1 is the critical path. Later orders are told `0004` is frozen and that a missing column is a scope question, not a `0005`. |
| 5 | The Master dispatched wave-1 implementers into worktrees branched from `main`, not from the brief branch, so none of them had its work order file. | Master error: the harness creates an agent worktree from the repository's default branch, not from the session's current branch, and the Master did not check before dispatching. Caught within one Master turn by inspecting `git worktree list`. | Nobody. This is a defect. | Four running agents were sent a correction telling them to `git merge --no-edit feat/brief-fr-006-nothing-missed` and to reconcile any work done on a guess against the order. Any work an implementer did before the correction was done without the Master decisions the order carries, so its return is judged against the order and not against what it thought it was building. |
| 6 | Work order **F2** hit its 60-turn budget with no `scripts/dev_env.py` written — 63 tool uses spent entirely on exploration. | Budget overruns are recorded, not extended silently (protocol §1). | Master. Nothing on the critical path waited — every other implementer creates its database with `psql` directly, which is why the order's fallback path exists. |
