| # | What | Why | Authorised by | Consequence |

| 1 | Track 0's `F2` was dispatched to an implementer rather than executed by the Master. | The brief calls Track 0 "sequential, Master"; the protocol's §0 says the Master never implements. These are different semantics, and claim A-18 (carried from FR-005 A-13) requires the filter vocabulary to keep working — including the Overseer's own Appendix-5 decision that `target_roles` defaults to `rank_only`, a mode facets cannot express. Collapsing the two tables would have deleted the vocabulary that the Overseer's decision is written in. | Master. | Two tables instead of one. `/api/filters` survives; `/api/facets` is new. The brief's stated intent — every extracted attribute is a facet with include/exclude/off and nothing hides by default — is delivered in full. |
| 4 | The whole of migration `0004` — including columns and tables that work orders A2, C1, C2, D2 and E4 will populate — is specified by the Master and written by work order **A1** alone. | The brief has five deliverables sharing one revision. Five implementers editing one migration file in five worktrees produces either a merge conflict or five revisions. | Master, per protocol §4 (the Master resolves integration). | A1 is the critical path. Later orders are told `0004` is frozen and that a missing column is a scope question, not a `0005`. |
| 5 | The Master dispatched wave-1 implementers into worktrees branched from `main`, not from the brief branch, so none of them had its work order file. | Master error: the harness creates an agent worktree from the repository's default branch, not from the session's current branch, and the Master did not check before dispatching. This is a defect. | Four running agents were sent a correction telling them to `git merge --no-edit feat/brief-fr-006-nothing-missed` and to reconcile any work done on a guess against the order.
